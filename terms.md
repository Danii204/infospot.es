## What you may do

Use InfoSpot for your own fishing and outdoor activities: look at the maps, the reservoir levels,
the weather and the estimated depth, and keep your own spots and tracks.

## The estimated depth maps

InfoSpot's own work: they were computed in a long, documented process from public terrain models,
satellite images and reservoir volumes. InfoSpot claims in them the right the law gives the maker of
a database (Directive 96/9/EC and articles 133 to 137 of the Spanish Intellectual Property Law), in
addition to any copyright. That right covers InfoSpot's maps, not the public data they are built

- redistribute, sell or publish the depth maps or a substantial part of them;
- get around the limits or protections of the app or the website.

Looking at the maps, asking for the depth at points as you fish and sharing a screenshot of where
you were are normal use. The depth maps carry identifying marks that make it possible to recognise
copies. InfoSpot keeps records of unusual use of its servers (see the [privacy
policy](https://infospot.es/privacy/)) and may defend its rights in court against anyone who copies
the maps in bulk.

## Not for navigation

InfoSpot is a beta. The estimated depth is an estimate made from satellite images and terrain
models; it has been checked against measured depths only at two points of Belesar, and it can be
several metres wrong, most of all where the bed was never seen dry. Do not use it, the map or the
GPS as your only navigation aid or for safety decisions on the water. Reservoir levels come from
official sources and can be late or wrong.
