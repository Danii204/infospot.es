InfoSpot

A fishing companion for the reservoirs of Spain. Android app in public beta.

  • Water level of 56 reservoirs, updated every week, with history since 1988.
  • Orthophotos, topographic maps and historical aerial photos, also offline.
  • Estimated depth (beta, not for navigation) on 42 reservoirs.
  • Your spots and tracks, which never leave your phone.
  • Wind, pressure, rain, sun and moon.
